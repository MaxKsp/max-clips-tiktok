Max Clips — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Information applicable to the currently available version.

Data processed

The desktop integration processes the TikTok account identifier and authorized access tokens, video files selected by the user and associated captions or publishing parameters. Your video editing assets are stored in local folders unless you explicitly choose to send content through a third-party service.

Purpose and consent

We use authorized TikTok data solely to display connection state and perform user-requested TikTok posting operations. The user can decline authorization. Uploads are initiated only after review and confirmation.

Storage and security

The desktop implementation encrypts account tokens at rest on the local device. Files and encryption keys remain under the device owner's control. Securing the device and its backups remains important.

Sharing

When you authorize publication, the selected video and metadata are transmitted to TikTok through its official API. External AI or media services, if enabled by the user, operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local files and credentials remain until deleted or disconnected by the device owner. Disconnecting the local account stops subsequent access through the application, but may not invalidate existing tokens at TikTok. TikTok data is governed by TikTok's separate policies.

Your rights

You may revoke application access through TikTok account settings and delete locally stored information. For privacy requests, contact maxkiller209@gmail.com.
